Data Engineer - 2025:2026


Deciphering transcriptomic changes in reef coral Stylophora pistillata between high dinoflagelates density zones (HDD) and low dinoflagellates density zones (LDD) such as the growing edge in light and dark conditions.
This project includes pipeline development, data analysis, interpretation and visual interface development.
Available here .
Alexander Venn - Physiology and Biochemistry team at the Scientific Center of Monaco.

PhD in Computational Biology - 2021:2024


Firstly, I enhanced the mitoXplorer 2.0 by incorporating a single-cell analysis section thereby creating version 3.0. This included the development of the web-based software linked to its database.
Additionally, I developed a sister platform called ataxiaXplorer, building its unique interactome by gathering RNA-seq data from various spinocerebellar ataxias and conducting analyses to identify shared genes.
The ultimate aim is to provide users with a tool to explore their data more comprehensively within the contexts of both ataxia and mitochondria.
Bianca Habermann - Computational Biology team at the IBDM.

Teaching - 2020:2024


I have been teaching R and mySQL in Master 1, Licence 2 & 3 writing, doing and correcting courses, trainings and exams.
Aix-Marseille Université - AMU.

Data Engineer - 2020:2021


I had the opportunity to pursue my Master 2 internship, which resulted in the publication of my RNA-seq analysis R-shiny App called RNfuzzyApp. During this year, I analyzed RNA-seq, ATAC-seq, DamID and CUT&RUN data and contributed to mitoXplorer 2.0 and Annominer development.
